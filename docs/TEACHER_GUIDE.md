# Instructor setup and classroom use

## Before distributing the URL

Review `MODEL.md` and the M parameters in `config.js`. The supplied sheet leaves savings volumes, business response, targeting and other parameters open. This release supplies transparent defaults so it can run immediately. They require pedagogical review; they are not newly verified country estimates.

The app uses four policy sections: subsidy reform (two pump prices), tax, household assistance, borrowing. The worksheets previously used a single subsidy-reduction field. Students should enter the two pump prices and the **modelled savings in Rp trillion** together in that worksheet’s field, or label the worksheet field “Fuel subsidy reform: petrol, diesel and modelled savings.” Do not describe modelled savings as a historically observed figure.

The final complication is the data sheet’s permitted budget-revision stress. It replaces the earlier guide’s unspecified oil-price increase. There is no Vietnam app in this package.

Agree and freeze assumptions for all groups before the three role rounds. Settings can be edited in Data & model for demonstration, but they are deliberately not password protected. For permanent defaults, edit `config.js`, update its version, and redeploy. Role-round records should use the same assumptions when comparing perspectives.

Tell students the private submission location and deadline. Explain that Save/Submit is local and downloads still need to be handed in. No software or student login is necessary.

## Teaching sequence

- Week 03: read starting information and model caveats, prepare, negotiate round 1, save group and individual explanations.
- Week 04: change to round 2, accept the reset and rotate roles. The dashboard returns to the original policy baseline.
- Week 05: repeat for round 3, using the same assumptions.
- Week 06: select Final. Show the budget revision context and fixed 6.1T teaching stress, then ask groups to revise or defend their package. Submit the final explanation and download evidence.
- Open the historical reveal only after a selected record is saved for the current stage. The reveal gate is instructional, not secure: all public JavaScript is inspectable.

Data & model contains calibration details and therefore eventual price information. Keep students in the Simulation tab during decisions if preserving the reveal sequence matters. A static public app cannot make this information secret.

## Interpretation and support

The starting budget is reconciled visibly because rounded source figures do not add exactly. The GDP ratio is a proxy. Borrowing changes financing, not the deficit. The higher-cost business and household examples leave out many behavioral and distributional effects, including tax incidence. Ask students to identify these omissions.

Allow simple English, bilingual terminology, notes, tables and diagrams. Students are assessed for explained relationships, evidence, stakeholder consequences, revision and awareness of assumptions, not for optimizing a dashboard score or reporting increased confidence.

Use the app’s exports alongside individual journals and examination work. Group outputs alone do not establish individual understanding. Journal 1 remains due at the end of Week 08 under the course plan.
