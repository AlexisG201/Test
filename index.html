Calibrated to 2013. This is a transparent teaching model, not a validated national forecast. The supplied data sheet is a draft; unresolved figures are not silently treated as confirmed facts.

From preparation to submission

  1. Enter the group code and the three student codes. Read the baseline and the role concerns.
  2. Prepare an individual recommendation, then negotiate. Use the controls to test alternatives.
  3. Write the group reasons and stakeholder effects. Save attempts only when useful.
  4. Each student saves their own role note. Submit the selected group record.
  5. Download a snapshot and the JSON/CSV evidence. Upload the files to your instructor’s course platform.
  6. For rounds 2 and 3, change stage. The policy and explanations reset; saved records remain. Roles rotate automatically.
  7. After round 3, select Final for the budget-revision stress. Reconsider the package and submit a final recommendation.
  8. Use your individual notes for Journal 1, due at the end of Week 08. Explain your own reasoning, even when the group agreed.

Working safely with your records

There are no accounts or automatic cloud submissions. Anyone using this browser profile may see its saved work. Download your files and clear local work before leaving a shared device. If browser storage is unavailable, the app will warn you; keep the tab open and download immediately. A printout is a fallback.

What is different in the final stage?

The data sheet permits reusing the official budget revision rather than inventing an oil shock. This version uses the reported Rp6.1 trillion rise in the fuel-subsidy allocation as a fixed extra cost in a classroom stress test. Oil-price and exchange-rate assumptions provide context. This is not a full replay of the revised budget or an estimate of their causal effects.

Stage and reveal controls guide classroom timing; they are not secure exam controls. Historical material is present in the public source files. This app has no student-facing AI, API key, live data feed, or external software dependency.
